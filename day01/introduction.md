# About Me

I'm Delfina, a researcher working at the intersection
of bioinformatics, computational biology,
and transcriptomics.

My research focuses on understanding cellular and
molecular mechanisms of human disease using single-cell
and spatial transcriptomics, with a particular interest
in liver vascular biology.

I use computational approaches to explore cellular
heterogeneity, gene expression, and cell–cell
interactions, transforming complex biological data
into meaningful scientific insights.
