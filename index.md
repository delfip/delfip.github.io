<h2 style="color:#127E87;">🔬 About Me</h2>

Welcome to my personal website!

I am a researcher interested in understanding the
cellular and molecular mechanisms underlying human diseases.

My work focuses on **single-cell and spatial transcriptomics**,
with a particular interest in **liver vascular biology**.

I enjoy combining computational biology, data analysis,
and biological research to investigate complex tissues.

---

<h2 style="color:#127E87;">🧬 Research Interests</h2>

- **Single-cell RNA sequencing:** Exploring cellular diversity and gene expression.
- **Spatial transcriptomics:** Understanding the organization of cells within tissues.
- **Liver vascular biology:** Investigating vascular changes in liver diseases.
- **Cell-cell communication:** Studying interactions between different cell populations.
- **Bioinformatics:** Analyzing and visualizing complex biological datasets.
